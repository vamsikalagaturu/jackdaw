# Privacy

jackdaw runs on your device. Your mail, calendar events, settings and sign-ins (passwords, and Google's sign-in when
you connect Google Calendar) are stored only there: settings in jackdaw's settings file, mail and events in its data
folder, passwords and sign-ins in the system keystore.

jackdaw sends data only to the servers you configure: your mail server (IMAP, SMTP, ManageSieve), your calendar server
(CalDAV, or Google Calendar), and, for remote images in mail you choose to load, the servers hosting them. When you
sign in with Google, Google's sign-in service is contacted to grant and renew calendar access.

The developer receives no data: there are no analytics, no telemetry and no servers of jackdaw's own. The daily update
check asks GitHub for the latest release and can be turned off in Settings.
